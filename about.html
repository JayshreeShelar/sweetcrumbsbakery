Our story

The story behind Sweet Crumbs

A small neighbourhood bakery that grew from one family kitchen and a big love of baking.

Since 2014

It began with one oven and a Sunday cake

Sweet Crumbs started in 2014, when Priya baked birthday cakes for friends from her home kitchen. Word spread, orders grew, and a year later the first small shop opened its doors.

Today we are a team of twelve, still working from the same recipes. We believe a good bake needs time, good butter and someone who cares how it turns out.

If it isn’t good enough for our own family’s table, it doesn’t leave the kitchen.

How we bake

Our baking philosophy

  • Fresh ingredients

    We buy butter, eggs, fruit and flour from nearby suppliers and use them while they are at their best.

  • Handmade quality

    Doughs rest overnight, pastry is folded by hand and every cake is finished one at a time. It takes longer, and you can taste the difference.
